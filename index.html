Settings

Dictate with this on, then download the zip and send it to Matthew. The count is segments, not dictations: the recorder cuts your speech at every pause of about a second (or at 25 seconds) and transcribes each piece as you go, so one note is usually 5 to 10 segments. The zip contains the audio of each segment and what the engine wrote for it. Do not include patient names or identifiers.

My vocabulary

One per line. Format: what Whisper hears = What it should write, for example parma partners = Parma partners.
Where this is saved: in this browser on this computer only. It survives closing the tab, but it does not follow you to another workstation and shared PCs may clear it at logoff. Terms you add here are also whispered to the speech engine before each sentence, so it learns to hear them. Export a copy to keep it, and email new terms to Matthew.Burstein@uhhospitals.org so they go into the shared dictionary for everyone. No patient data belongs here.

Tips

  • Suture sizes format automatically: "three oh Vicryl" becomes 3-0 Vicryl, "number one PDS" becomes #1 PDS.
  • Speak in phrases and pause briefly; each pause is transcribed as a unit. Text appears a second or two behind you.
  • Click anywhere in the note to move the cursor, then dictate; text inserts at the cursor.
  • F2 starts and stops recording. Ctrl+Shift+C copies the note. Ctrl+Z works in the text box.
  • The note stays in this browser tab until you clear it, even if you refresh. Clear it when you're done.
  • Add your own terms under Settings → My vocabulary.
  • Bugs, misheard terms, or feature requests: email Matthew Burstein at Matthew.Burstein@uhhospitals.org.
